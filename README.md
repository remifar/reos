## English

### What is rEos?

**rEos** is a modular, multi-tenant SaaS Enterprise Operating System for businesses of every size — from a small grocery/POS to a multinational enterprise. A small, secure **Rust kernel** powers universal engines for identity, parties, items, sales, inventory, accounting, tax, workflows, and AI.

Country, industry, and business-model capabilities install as signed plugins through APIs — never touching the core.

**Open to use. Plugin-extensible. Built for long-term stability.**

## नेपाली

### rEos के हो?

**rEos** एउटा modular, multi-tenant SaaS Enterprise Operating System हो — सानो किराना/POS व्यवसायदेखि बहुराष्ट्रिय संस्थासम्मका लागि। यसको स्थायी आधार एउटा सानो, सुरक्षित **Rust Kernel** हो, जसले identity, party, item, sales, inventory, accounting, tax, workflow र AI जस्ता universal engines सञ्चालन गर्छ।

Country, industry र business-model क्षमताहरू signed plugin को रूपमा API मार्फत जोडिन्छन् — core लाई कहिल्यै छुँदैन।

**प्रयोगका लागि खुला। Plugin-विस्तारयोग्य। दीर्घकालीन स्थिरताका लागि निर्मित।**

## Why rEos? | rEos किन?

**English**

- **Small, stable kernel** — deterministic and secure.
- **Universal business core** — Party, Item, Order, Invoice, Payment, Inventory, Accounting.
- **Composable extensions** — Country, Industry, Business Model.
- **Organization configuration** — personalize without forking.
- **User/Role policy** — who can do what, everywhere.
- **AI at the edges** — proposes and orchestrates; kernel validates and guarantees.

**नेपाली**

- **सानो र स्थिर Kernel** — deterministic र सुरक्षित।
- **Universal business core** — Party, Item, Order, Invoice, Payment, Inventory, Accounting।
- **जोड्न मिल्ने extensions** — Country, Industry, Business Model।
- **Organization configuration** — source-code fork नगरी कम्पनीअनुसार तयार।
- **User/Role policy** — कसले के गर्न पाउँछ, सबैतिर निर्धारण।
- **AI किनारामा** — प्रस्ताव र orchestration गर्छ; Kernel ले validate र guarantee गर्छ।
